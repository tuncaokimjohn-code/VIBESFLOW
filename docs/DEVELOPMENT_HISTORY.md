# VibeFlow Inspector AI — Dated development history

**Documentary cutoff:** 5 November 2026 (PHT). **Project:** VibeFlow Inspector AI. **Tagline:** Built by a vibe coder, for vibe coders.

## Purpose and interpretation

This chronology is based on dated planning files, named candidate directories, builder reports, QA handoffs, preservation manifests, available project conversation continuity, and the supplied demonstration video. It records **engineering activity and the evidence available**, not an assertion that every candidate was accepted. Historical conversations not fully exported are not represented as verbatim transcripts.
